# V6 implementation review

The main v5 limitation was missing mechanisms/candidates, alongside imperfect balance state. Increasing model capacity would not create a card-order reversal, interpret an unparsed advance clause or supply a missing credit-note group. V6 addresses those sources directly and preserves the fitted ranker as the incumbent.

Reviewed contracts: positive item-currency amounts including credit notes; signed arithmetic only inside cash identities; per-transfer rather than whole-run returns; gross card order rather than net settlement; service period rather than booking month; advance cash marked PARTIAL; old-reference routing to an issued replacement; no duplicate refund confused with an APC payout.

The broad fee experiment was too aggressive: it overwrote withholding decisions and selected old nearby invoices. The shipped rule preserves known withholding/discount/dispute decisions, requires cross-border/fee evidence and excludes unreferenced stale invoices. Regression tests cover that interaction. Template retrieval is preferable to a blanket recall threshold change.

The final cached development comparison is 81.41 vs v5 79.29. Of 145 changed bank lines, 139 become correct, none previously correct become wrong, and six remain wrong by the official transaction check. These results come from an inspected development fixture, including rule selection on that fixture. They are not a new holdout or a LIVE score. Rare-family sample sizes and the incomplete historical TRAIN ledger constrain the conclusion.

The replacement/credit guard checks that the replacement exists by the bank booking date. Source returns require source dates no later than the return and exact currency/cash agreement. No golden is read in the LIVE path. Archive/catalog/text-cache dependencies are documented; this is an incremental cached release, not a new clean-room re-extraction pipeline.

12 new mechanism tests and 33 existing v5 tests pass. Release checks use the real LIVE bank registry and item catalog, plus the organizer's parser. Aggregate prediction-ledger conflicts stay visible diagnostics under the previous competition validation profile; hard schema, membership, amount and coverage errors block release. The final release report records the full conflict count against v5.

Remaining work after website feedback: large reference-free groups, partial/remainder episodes with missing prior history, and missing source context. Do not promise a 90 score from synthetic structure alone. Keep the new edition immutable, compare low-family gains and high-performing AP controls, then decide the next scope.

Release verified: 203,083/203,083 LIVE lines, 643,231 rows, no hard errors, official schema accepted. Aggregate over-clearing diagnostics decrease from v5's 16,991 to 16,758.
